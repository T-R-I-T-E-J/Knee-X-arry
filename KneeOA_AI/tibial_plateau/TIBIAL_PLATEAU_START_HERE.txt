🔍 WHAT THE SYSTEM DETECTS & MEASURES

THE 3 FIXED POINTS:
───────────────────
  ① MEDIAL EDGE (Left)
     - Innermost boundary of tibial plateau
     - Pixel coordinate: x_min of plateau contour
     - Example: x = 142px in your first X-ray
  
  ② CENTRAL EMINENCE (Center) ← DIVISION POINT
     - Peak in the middle of plateau
     - Pixel coordinate: (x_min + x_max) / 2
     - Example: x = 282px in your first X-ray
     - A VERTICAL LINE is drawn through this point
  
  ③ LATERAL EDGE (Right)
     - Outermost boundary of tibial plateau
     - Pixel coordinate: x_max of plateau contour
     - Example: x = 423px in your first X-ray


THE MEASUREMENTS:
─────────────────
  MEDIAL HALF (Left of division line):
    - 4 measurement points at 20%, 40%, 60%, 80% positions
    - Each point: perpendicular distance (femur to tibia) = JSW
    - Report: min, max, mean ± std dev (in millimeters)
    - Example: 3.2, 3.5, 3.4, 3.1 mm → Mean = 3.3mm (Grade 0-1)
  
  LATERAL HALF (Right of division line):
    - 4 measurement points at 20%, 40%, 60%, 80% positions
    - Each point: perpendicular distance (femur to tibia) = JSW
    - Report: min, max, mean ± std dev (in millimeters)
    - Example: 3.6, 3.8, 3.7, 3.5 mm → Mean = 3.65mm (Grade 0-1)


CLINICAL GRADES (Based on Mean JSW):
────────────────────────────────────
  Grade 0 (Normal):       JSW > 5.0 mm
  Grade 1 (Doubtful):     JSW 4.0-5.0 mm
  Grade 2 (Minimal OA):   JSW 3.0-4.0 mm
  Grade 3 (Moderate OA):  JSW 1.5-3.0 mm
  Grade 4 (Severe OA):    JSW < 1.5 mm

⚙️  TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE

8-Step Processing Pipeline:
───────────────────────────

INPUT: Knee X-ray image
  ↓
1. Image Enhancement (denoise, contrast equalization)
  ↓
2. Identify Knee Region (ROI)
  ↓
3. Detect Tibial Plateau (edge detection, contour analysis)
  ↓
4. Identify Fixed Points (medial, center, lateral edges)
  ↓
5. Draw Division Line (vertical through central eminence)
  ↓
6. Detect Femoral Surfaces (for JSW measurement reference)
  ↓
7. Measure JSW at 4 Points per Half (perpendicular distances)
  ↓
8. Validate & Confidence Score (plausibility checks)
  ↓
OUTPUT: Structured measurements + visual overlays

🎓 MEDICAL BACKGROUND (If Needed)

Kellgren-Lawrence Grade System:
  • Used worldwide for standardizing OA severity
  • Based on X-ray findings (JSW, osteophytes, sclerosis)
  • 5 grades: 0 (normal) to 4 (severe)
  • JSW measurement is primary indicator

Your Doctor's Request:
  • Divide plateau into medial and lateral halves
  • Measure JSW in each half separately
  • Useful for:
    - Detecting asymmetric OA (one side worse)
    - Tracking disease progression
    - Treatment planning
    - Follow-up comparison
